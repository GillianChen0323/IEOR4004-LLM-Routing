DERIVED DATASET AND LIMITS
Our CSV is a source-derived subset, not a newly collected benchmark and not the full RouterBench.
All three models' historical evaluation scores and costs come from RouterBench, on matching rows.
Source: 36,497 rows -> selected benchmarks: 2,361 -> exact-prompt deduplication: 2,359.
Columns: sample_id, eval_name, category, three score columns, three |total_cost columns.
Model pool: gpt-3.5-turbo-1106, gpt-4-1106-preview, mistralai/mixtral-8x7b-chat.
No new LLM calls were made. Historical costs are not today's API price quotes.
M3 categories are retained; the M4 benchmark mapping is explicit and is not a claim that M3
specified those exact benchmark names. Reasoning = formal logic + logical fallacies.
Calibration/heldout split: 1,649 / 710, seed 4004; five paired bootstrap scenarios use only calibration.
Source and derived CSV hashes are in data/provenance.json; the split manifest is in results/.
No source license or permission is invented here; consult the source pages and underlying datasets
before broader redistribution. This package provides attribution and a compact research subset.
